# LoanAndGo

## Problem Statement
Applying for a loan can be complicated and frustration for a average joe. Customer would often need to rely on sales agents, bank agents or searching online on the loan application process, what documents that are needed, and what are the requirements for the loan approval. Even after submitting all the documents, the wait for the loan status often makes the applicants anxious, as they would need to plan for future arrangements, or if their loan application is rejected, they would need to quickly submit another application to another bank. Furthermore, any delays due to incomplete documents submitted or background of the applicants that may require further check may frustrate the applicant since the status is mostly undisclosed and requires further communications between the applicants and the banks, which is susceptible to miscommunication, or arguments happening. 

At the same time, banks must manually review large volumes of applications, verify submitted information, identify missing documents, and assess applicants' financial circumstances. These administrative tasks consume valuable resources, reduce operational efficiency, and may slow down the overall loan application process. 

Existing digital application channels may simplify online submissions, but applicants can still struggle to understand their requirements and prepare complete applications before submitting them for review.

LoanAndGo aims to address these challenges by providing an AI-powered, one-stop loan application assistant that guides applicants through the application process, checks document readiness, provides preliminary affordability insights, and uses predictive machine learning to support credit-risk assessment. By connecting an Applicant Portal with a Bank Officer Dashboard, LoanAndGo seeks to reduce avoidable administrative delays, improve application completeness, and help financial institutions organise and review applications more efficiently. The goal is to create a faster, more accessible, and more transparent loan application experience while supporting responsible lending practices. LoanAndGo is designed to assist applicants and bank officers, not replace official bank underwriting or final loan approval decisions.
